Help with the Hane dictation app for Windows

For technical support, privacy questions or feedback, email:

When reporting a problem

It helps to include:

  • your Windows version
  • the Hane version
  • a short description of what happened and what you expected

Please do not send API keys, passwords, microphone recordings or confidential dictated text.
