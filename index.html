Hane. Speak naturally.
Type effortlessly.

Hane is a push-to-talk dictation app for Windows. Hold your shortcut, speak, and let go. Hane turns your speech into text on your computer and types it into the app you are using.

  • Transcribed on your computer

    Speech recognition runs locally, using a model included with Hane. Your microphone audio is not uploaded.

  • Optional cloud cleanup

    Off unless you turn it on. When it is on, Hane sends the transcript text, never the audio, to Anthropic, OpenAI or Google, using your own API key, to improve punctuation and fix obvious transcription mistakes.

  • Your personal dictionary

    Tell Hane how to write words it keeps getting wrong. Your corrections are applied on your computer.

Requires Windows 10 version 2004 or later on an x64 PC.
